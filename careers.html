Careers

We're building a programming language where programs are alive. If that's the kind of problem you want to work on, we want to talk.

Flat structure. You set your hours, you own your work, and we handle the rest.

Everyone here is a W-2 employee, never a contractor.

How We Work

Weekly check-ins

Clock in and out whenever you like. Once a week, a quick check-in with your hours and a line on where the project stands. Every hour you log is an hour we pay for, so the work you record and the work you get paid for are always the same thing.

Monthly one-on-ones

At the end of each month, a one-on-one where we go over everything together and make sure you have what you need to move at your own pace.

Your hours, your pace

You decide how much time you can give, and the schedule follows. More time and effort means faster growth.

Work from anywhere

Work from the office, from home, or from anywhere. Meeting in person is optional, for when it helps.

What We Offer

Equity for early hires

Meaningful stake from day one. Your shares vest as the company grows.

W-2 employment

Payroll taxes, workers' comp, and the protections of employment, not contractor paperwork.

Paid for the work you do

Hours logged are hours paid, with bonuses that recognize contributions the whole team can see.

Ship real things

Working code beats slides. Every person changes the product, and the first users will run it.

Grow with the company

Senior seats grow into their own divisions. Early contributors grow with the work they take on.

Room to do it right

No rush to ship something wrong. The product earns its place by being correct, and so does the work.

How We Hire

1
You reach out

Email us with what you've built, what you want to work on, and why. Links to real things you've shipped carry more weight than a resume. If you have a resume, attach it, but we care about the work first.

2
First call

30 minutes, informal. We talk through your background, what we're building at VolkanaDEV, and whether the fit makes sense on both sides. No whiteboard, no leetcode, no trick questions.

3
Working session

A longer conversation focused on your area of expertise. We'll walk through a real problem we're working on and see how you approach it. This is collaborative, not a test. We want to see how you think, not whether you get the "right" answer.

4
Reference calls

We talk to a couple people you've worked with before. People who can tell us what it's actually like to build something alongside you. Not HR checks, just honest conversations.

5
Offer and onboarding

If it's a fit, we move quickly. You'll get the flat employment agreement to review, we'll walk through it together, and you start when you're ready. No waiting weeks for a decision that was made in the first call.

Don't See Your Role?

If you believe in what we're building and have skills we need, write anyway. Tell us what you can do, what you've built, and why Sanguis matters to you.
