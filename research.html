We publish the work behind every claim.

Three papers are live on Zenodo, each with a DOI, and every result in them was re-run on the dates recorded inside. They read in order: the foundation, the core, then the application.

The papers

1 · Foundation

Independent execution pipelines of one language agree bit-for-bit on chaotic workloads

Two separate ways of running the same Sanguis program produce byte-identical output, on workloads where a difference of one bit would explode. This is the floor the rest of the work stands on.

Wolfe, A. · 2026 · 10.5281/zenodo.23277353

2 · Core

A pair-primary associative substrate with exact in-band episodic memory

The substrate itself: memory whose unit is a relation, reading back what it has lived exactly, with every condition stated in the paper.

Wolfe, A. · 2026 · 10.5281/zenodo.23276767

3 · Application

A pair-primary associative substrate with route-unique composition

Composition over the substrate: each route seeded by its own pair, so composed output is pair-specific and verifiably free of leaks.

Wolfe, A. · 2026 · 10.5281/zenodo.23277683

Published under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

How it's made

Every paper says how it was made, and so does every record.

This statement appears in all three papers, in the same words:

"The system and efforts producing the results of this document are the author's design, built and run over the past eight months. AI assistants contributed throughout: initial drafts of the prose, insight, suggested sources, and supervised code execution to detect and patch flaws during the experiments. Every cited source was independently checked before use, and all programs were verified running in a closed environment without AI assistance. Every result reproduces from the scripts and dates recorded in the paper."

The scripts behind the results are not included in the preprints. They release with the project.
